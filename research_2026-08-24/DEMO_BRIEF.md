# Potential Cold Demo brief — not authorised to build

`DEMO STATUS: NOT AUTHORISED.`  
This is a factual brief only; no HTML, CSS, JavaScript, asset download, deployment or public URL is produced.

## Single-page purpose

Help a resident or small-business visitor understand the verified cleaning offer and choose one direct contact route without creating a booking system, customer form or price-maintenance burden.

## Direction

**Working concept:** “A clear local cleaning introduction, built around the services and proof already visible on the business’s owned site.”

The page should be warm, practical and restrained—not luxury-hotel, chemical-lab or generic marketplace. Use a clean editorial structure with a warm muted clay accent only after brand review. Do not treat the existing generic yellow-glove hero as a JL and TM business photograph.

## Proposed architecture

1. **Hero:** exact business name; a factual, non-geographically overbroad service line; one primary `Call or email` CTA. No invented availability, response time or booking language.
2. **What we help with:** three small, evidence-backed cards: routine home cleaning; deep/move-related cleaning; commercial cleaning or a confirmed alternative. Keep unconfirmed offerings out.
3. **Why customers choose the service:** family-run / training / certification / insurance only if reconfirmed; otherwise use a neutral “current customer feedback” pointer without quote or rating fabrication.
4. **A recent customer voice:** use no quote until rights and exact wording are confirmed. The current 10 August 2026 review is research evidence, not automatically reusable Demo copy.
5. **Simple contact strip:** publicly displayed phone and email only, with service-area wording held until resolved.
6. **Bottom 40% commercial architecture:** proof/context → service fit reminder → single contact CTA → short practical contact details. This retains a business conversation path without a form or booking flow.

## Mobile strategy

- Business name wraps fully; never crop or overflow.
- A single persistent contact affordance can reveal only the pre-verified call/email actions.
- One-column service cards, readable review/trust copy, and no large navigation list.
- Do not cover the opening content with internal banners, cookie-choice simulation or an unverified booking widget.

## Reserved for a later, expressly authorised production conversation

- Exact geographic coverage and travel terms.
- Customer-supplied or authorised real-work photography.
- Final pricing, minimum durations, quote conditions and availability.
- Quote/review permission and third-party badge/award permission.
- Any booking platform link, form, payment, social workflow, analytics, cookie configuration, CMS or continuing updates.

## Scope control

The potential Demo is a static presentation of selected verified material only. It must not represent a live service, use a fake form, claim to accept bookings, issue quotes, process payments, show availability, or promise routine content updates.
