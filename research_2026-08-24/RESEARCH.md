## Static-scope and risk boundary

Permissible £600-page direction: static HTML/CSS/limited front-end JS; business introduction, selected verified services, proof already public, contact details and a telephone/email CTA. Existing telephone/email can remain the action path.

Do **not** include or promise: CMS/admin, server-side enquiry form, self-hosted booking, calendar availability, payment, checkout, inventory, customer accounts, ongoing price updates, review-feed integration, analytics/tracking, or automated quote logic. The current site visually exposes a “Book” control and a message form, but the linked destination/operational ownership was not inspected or used; therefore any booking integration is `OUT OF SCOPE / UNKNOWN`.
