# Design Intent — Revision 3 / internal

**Design Read:** A straightforward, well-composed local-service homepage for a Merthyr Tydfil cleaning team: real work evidence, legible service scope and direct human contact—not a fashion editorial or a generic cleaning template.

**Business cues:** the unaltered official JL & TM logo; the verified Merthyr Tydfil location, telephone, public Gmail and Monday–Saturday 08:00–18:00 hours; genuine carpet-equipment, home-work, commercial-work and paired oven images; and the actual household/deep/move/carpet/commercial service mix.

**Audience and page job:** a household or small-workplace visitor should immediately recognise the service, see one genuine result, understand the relevant type of job, and call or email. There is no booking workflow, form, price, coverage-radius promise or fabricated trust claim.

**Industry grammar:** crisp local service utility. The logo is shown in a cropped display window without altering the source asset; the headline is short, sans-serif and readable; the hero is a balanced service-copy/photo composition; evidence is shown as a clearly labelled pair; the contact close resembles an operating desk, not a poster CTA.

**Direction G — selected:** `Service clarity with work proof`. A contained official logo in a compact header → copy-first hero with a single real carpet-work photograph on the right → genuine oven before/after proof → three plain service rows → two real job images → a direct call/email/hours closure. Bottom 40% gives every visitor an operational reason and route to make contact.

**Direction H — rejected:** `Proof-led split screen`. An oven before/after pair would dominate the opening and lead to a slimmer service desk → one service list → broader carpet/commercial image band → direct contact close. It is rejected because the user’s screenshot demonstrates that oversized raw imagery should not bear the entire first impression; real proof works better after a clear business introduction.

**Dials:** `DESIGN_VARIANCE = 5`, `MOTION_INTENSITY = 2`, `VISUAL_DENSITY = 5`. The only animation allowed is a 180ms button colour/transform feedback, disabled when reduced motion is requested. There is no automatic reveal, scroll animation, video or carousel.

**Avoid:** square-logo canvas shown uncropped; display serif fragments; photo captions pasted over the image as decoration; image-first pages with insufficient service context; gloved-hand stock; invented staff, testimonials, reviews, guarantees, prices, service radius or project stories; and identical card grids.

**Imagegen outcome:** a generated ambient carpet texture was reviewed as a colour/composition reference only. It is not a JL and TM job, is not copied into this Demo, and does not replace any genuine customer work image.

**CROSS-DEMO SIMILARITY CHECK:** Direction G is recognisable by its verified small-team cleaning scope, paired oven evidence, carpet-equipment image, local operating hours and direct contact close. It does not reuse peer code, copy, marks, images or the section pattern of other workspace demos.
