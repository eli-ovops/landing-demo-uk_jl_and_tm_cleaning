## Synthesis applied to this concept

1. **Decisive first screen:** location, service scope, public contact route and a bounded review marker are visible together.
2. **Service board, not generic cards:** visitors choose a practical cleaning need from three groups whose underlying services are shown on JL & TM’s current site.
3. **Proof does visual work:** “13 reviews shown today” is a current owned-site count, not a rating, endorsement or review quote.
4. **Own-public-assets over stock polish:** two public business-gallery images are used with their source/rights boundary logged in `IMAGE_LEDGER.md`.
5. **Static-scope translation:** all peer-site booking, postcode search, instant pricing, forms, payment, calendars, chat and accounts are explicitly excluded.

## Verification limitation

Housekeep, MOLLY MAID, Bright & Beautiful, Maid2Clean, MSG Cleaners, Hello Cleaners and CMH Cleaning were structurally inspected in depth on 2026-08-24. Some other live sites returned partial rendering/errors to the review tool, so their row records observable public page patterns only, not unverified visual detail.

## Original direction

**Work that shows up.** Deep navy and electric blue are derived from JL & TM’s actual public logo; oversized editorial typography, a bounded evidence stamp, service rows and two real business-gallery images create an original composition. It is not a clone of any peer.
